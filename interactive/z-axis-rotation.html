A geometric derivation

Rotate the basis, read the columns.

A positive active rotation adds the same angle to both in-plane basis vectors. Their projections onto the fixed axes become the entries of the rotation matrix.

The one fact to remember A unit vector at azimuth φ has Cartesian components (cos φ, sin φ). The x basis starts at 0; the y basis starts at π/2.
